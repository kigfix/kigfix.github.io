How we pick

Nothing here is magic. It's a checklist applied the same way every matchday.

Recent form

Results over the last five to eight games, weighted toward home and away form separately, plus the quality of the opposition faced.

Chance creation

Expected goals for and against, shots allowed, and whether recent scorelines flatter or hide how a team has actually played.

Team news

Injuries, suspensions, rotation before big fixtures, travel and fixture congestion. We recheck lineups before kickoff when we can.

Price check

A pick only goes up if our estimated chance beats the chance implied by the odds. That gap is the "value" tag you see on a pick.

Questions

Are these predictions guaranteed?

No. Football is low-scoring and full of upsets. Our goal is to find picks where the odds undersell the real chance, and to show you the results either way. Some days will lose.

Is it really free?

Yes. Predictions, reasoning and the track record are free to read. There's no paywall and no "VIP" tier.

What does the confidence rating mean?

It's how sure we are about our own estimate, from 1 (shaky, limited information) to 5 (strong evidence from several angles). It is not a guarantee, and a 5 can still lose.

What does "value" mean?

If we think a team has a 62% chance to win but the odds imply 57%, there's a gap in your favour. That's value. A pick can have a high chance but no value if the odds are already very short.

Which leagues do you cover?

Mostly the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1 and the Champions League, because team news and data are easiest to check there.
